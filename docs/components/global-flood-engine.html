Global Flood Engine

GlobalFlood

Simulates global terrain flooding based on a target water volume and generates a depth heatmap.

Description

Calculates the water level required to fill a given volume across a terrain mesh. Output includes the final water elevation and a heatmap representing depth.

Inputs

NameTypeAccessDescription
TerrainMeshMeshItemInput Terrain Mesh
TargetVolumeNumberItemTarget volume of water
ZStepNumberItemIteration step for Z level (default: 0.1)

Outputs

NameTypeAccessDescription
FloodMeshMeshItemFlooded terrain heatmap mesh
WaterLevelNumberItemFinal water elevation
