WaterFlow

Generates a parametric grid, projects it to the terrain, and simulates downhill flow paths.

Description

Simulates water drop flow paths over a terrain mesh, tracing the exact geometric descent based on face normals and gravity.

Inputs

NameTypeAccessDescription
TerrainMeshMeshItemThe unified topological surface.
GridSpacingNumberItemThe XY distance between starting raindrops.
StepSizeNumberItemDistance the water travels per tick.
MaxStepsIntegerItemSafety limit for the simulation loop.

Outputs

NameTypeAccessDescription
FlowPathsCurveTreeThe generated water movement paths.
DropPointsPointTreeThe valid starting grid points on the mesh.
