Curve Segment Dispatcher

Curve Nickname: SegDisp

Overview

Explodes curves into Lines and Arcs, extracting Radii, Centers, and visual Dimensions.

Inputs (4)

  • curves (Curve): Curves to process
  • offset (Number): Dimension offset
  • dim_toggle (Boolean): Show dimensions
  • num_dec (Integer): Number of decimals

Outputs (5)

  • lines (Curve): Linear segments
  • arcs (Curve): Arc segments
  • radii (Number): Arc radii
  • centers (Point): Arc centers
  • dims (Generic): Visual dimensions

Method, assumptions and simplifications

The same notes appear in the component's Info output.

Method

  1. Splits each curve into segments (DuplicateSegments), keeping the branch structure.
  2. Linear segments go to lines. Other segments that are arcs within 0.001 go to arcs, with their radius and centre.
  3. With dim_toggle, each arc gets a radial leader line of length offset (default 10) outwards from the arc midpoint and a text dot R radius, rounded to num_dec decimals.

Assumptions

  • Curves are made of lines and arcs; offset is in model units.

Simplifications and limits

  • Segments that are neither lines nor arcs are not output; a warning gives their count.
