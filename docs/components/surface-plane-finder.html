Surface Plane Finder

Finds a plane from a planar surface or brep face.

Facade Nickname: plane_finder

Overview

Finds a plane from a planar surface or brep face.

Inputs (1)

  • x (Geometry): Input geometry

Outputs (3)

  • plane_normal (Vector): Normal of the plane
  • reverse_normal (Vector): Reversed normal
  • output_plane (Plane): Output plane

Method, assumptions and simplifications

The same notes appear in the component's Info output.

Method

  1. Takes the first item of x. A Surface is used directly; a single-face Brep uses its face's underlying surface; polysurfaces are rejected.
  2. If the surface is planar within the document absolute tolerance, the normal is evaluated at the middle of its UV domain, and the origin is the bounding-box centre (plane surfaces) or the area centroid of the untrimmed surface. Outputs the normal, its reverse and a plane at that origin.

Assumptions

  • The input is a single planar face.

Simplifications and limits

  • Only the first list item is processed.
  • The plane X axis is chosen by Rhino from the normal; it is not aligned with the face edges.

Workflow

  • Facade panel faces -> x; output_plane -> orientation of panels, attractors or text.
