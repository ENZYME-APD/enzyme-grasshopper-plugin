Terrain Grader

Generates adaptive grading meshes, volumes, and crisp cut/fill colors.

Site Analysis Nickname: TERRAIN GRADER

Overview

Generates adaptive grading meshes, volumes, and crisp cut/fill colors.

Inputs (8)

  • Mesh (Mesh): Original Topography
  • BoundaryCurves (Curve): Closed pads (Optional)
  • BlendAngle (Number): Max allowable slope in degrees
  • CutColor (Colour): Cut zones color
  • FillColor (Colour): Fill zones color
  • MeshResolution (Number): Base grid size
  • ShowContours (Boolean): Toggle contour generation
  • Run (Boolean): Optional. False = skip the analysis and keep showing the last results (cached). Share one toggle with the Analysis Dashboard to switch everything on/off together.

Outputs (7)

  • ModMesh (Mesh): Adaptive Tri-Mesh
  • ColoredMesh (Mesh): Unwelded crisp cut/fill visualizer
  • CutVolume (Number): Total Cut (Negative Z)
  • FillVolume (Number): Total Fill (Positive Z)
  • Contours (Curve): 1m Interval Curves
  • MainContours (Curve): 5m Interval Curves
  • Info (Text): Component information and interpretation

Method, assumptions and simplifications

The same notes appear in the component's Info output.

Method

  1. Each closed BoundaryCurves pad gets a best-fit plane through its polyline vertices (or 20 division points if it is not a polyline); open curves are ignored. With no pads the input Mesh is passed through unchanged with zero volumes.
  2. The terrain is sampled by vertical rays on a square grid of MeshResolution over the mesh bounding box. Where the local face is steeper than 30°, two extra samples are added at half a cell in X and in Y. Points every MeshResolution × 0.25 along each pad boundary are added too.
  3. A sample inside a pad (plan containment test in World XY) is moved onto that pad plane. A sample outside all pads is clamped into a slope envelope: for each pad, Z must lie within padZ ± d × tan(BlendAngle), where d is the plan distance to the nearest point on the pad boundary; the tightest limits over all pads apply.
  4. ModMesh is a 2D Delaunay triangulation (in World XY) of all the moved samples.
  5. CutVolume and FillVolume are summed on the base grid: at each cell centre the original and graded surfaces are ray-cast, and the height difference × MeshResolution² is counted as fill if above +0.01 m or as cut if below −0.01 m. Both are reported as positive volumes.
  6. ColoredMesh is an unwelded copy of ModMesh. Each face takes FillColor if the mean vertex height difference to the original terrain is above +0.05 m, CutColor if below −0.05 m, and white otherwise.
  7. With ShowContours on, ModMesh is contoured at every whole model unit between the original terrain bounding box limits; levels divisible by 5 go to MainContours, the rest to Contours.

Assumptions

  • Model units are metres: thresholds of 0.01 m and 0.05 m, the 30° refinement angle and the 1 m and 5 m contour intervals are hard-coded.
  • Pads are closed curves that are roughly planar; a sloping pad is allowed and follows its fitted plane.
  • The terrain is a single-valued height field seen from above (no overhangs); faces wound downwards are treated as upward for the steepness test.
  • BlendAngle is the steepest permitted embankment slope for both cut and fill (same value either side).

Simplifications and limits

  • Embankments are a geometric slope envelope (a cone of slope BlendAngle around the pads), not a designed batter, retaining wall or drainage profile; no soil type, bulking or shrinkage factor is applied.
  • The graded surface is a re-triangulation of grid samples, so away from the pads it is a linear resampling of the original terrain. On rough terrain this resampling error can show up as small cut or fill where nothing was graded (partly hidden by the 0.01 m threshold).
  • Volumes use one sample per grid cell (midpoint rule), so accuracy depends on MeshResolution.
  • Overlapping pads are resolved by input order (the first containing pad wins); conflicting envelopes from several pads are not reconciled.

Workflow

  • Site terrain mesh (for example from Site Fetch) -> Mesh; building pads or platforms as closed curves -> BoundaryCurves.
  • ColoredMesh for cut and fill diagrams; CutVolume and FillVolume for an early earthworks balance; Contours and MainContours for drawings.
