Facade Module: Spandrels

Generates solid horizontal spandrel bands, vision glass, and variable-depth mullions.

JSON Masterplan (Beta) Nickname: Mod_Spandrel

Overview

Generates solid horizontal spandrel bands, vision glass, and variable-depth mullions.

Inputs (8)

  • Bounds (Curve): Bounds
  • Heights (Number): Heights
  • BayWidth (Number): BayWidth
  • SillHeight (Number): SillHeight
  • HeaderDrop (Number): HeaderDrop
  • MullionDepth (Number): MullionDepth
  • FullHeightMullions (Boolean): FullHeightMullions
  • FlipDir (Boolean): FlipDir

Outputs (4)

  • Glass (Brep): Glass
  • Spandrels (Brep): Spandrels
  • Mullions (Brep): Mullions
  • Info (Text): Method, assumptions, simplifications and references.

Method, assumptions and simplifications

The same notes appear in the component's Info output.

Method

  1. Each curve in a branch is one floor outline, with floor height from the matching item of Heights (4.0 m if missing).
  2. The whole outline is extruded into a sill band of SillHeight, a glass band from SillHeight to height − HeaderDrop, and a header band of HeaderDrop; sill and header go to Spandrels.
  3. Each outline segment is divided into round(length / BayWidth) equal bays; at every division point (duplicates removed, 1 mm rounding) a mullion fin of MullionDepth is placed perpendicular to the segment in plan.
  4. Fins span the glass height, or the full floor height when FullHeightMullions is True; FlipDir turns them to the other side.
  5. The HUD reports glass and solid areas as outline length × band height and the mullion count.

Assumptions

  • Outlines are planar, horizontal and placed at each floor level; units are metres.
  • Fin side depends on segment direction; FlipDir reverses it.

Simplifications and limits

  • All elements are zero-thickness surfaces; mullions are single fins without width.
  • Glass is one continuous band per floor; bays only position the mullions.

Workflow

  • Masterplan or Facade Parser floor outlines and heights -> Bounds and Heights; outputs -> custom previews or BIM export.
