Enzyme Heliodon

Site Analysis Nickname: Heliodon

Overview

Generates a 3D solar dome and calculates annual sun vectors for environmental analysis.

Inputs (7)

  • Latitude (Number): Location latitude (-90 to 90)
  • Longitude (Number): Location longitude (-180 to 180)
  • Time Zone (Number): Time zone offset from GMT
  • Radius (Number): Visual radius of the heliodon dome
  • Center (Point): Center point of the heliodon
  • Months (Interval): Domain of months to evaluate (e.g., 1 to 12).
  • Hours (Interval): Domain of hours to evaluate (e.g., 8 to 17).

Outputs (4)

  • Sun Vectors (Vector): All solar vectors above the horizon (pointing TO the sun).
  • Daily Arcs (Curve): Visual sun path curves for the 21st of each month.
  • Sun Points (Point): Visual hourly sun positions.
  • Base Circle (Curve): A flat circle framing the heliodon radius.
