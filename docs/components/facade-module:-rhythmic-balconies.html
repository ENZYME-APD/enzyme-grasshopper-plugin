Facade Module: Rhythmic Balconies

2D patterned balconies with merge logic, corner isolation, and fast math.

JSON Masterplan (Beta) Nickname: Mod_Rhythm_Balc

Overview

2D patterned balconies with merge logic, corner isolation, and fast math.

Inputs (14)

  • Bounds (Curve): Curve boundaries
  • Heights (Number): Heights
  • BayWidth (Number): Bay width
  • Depth (Number): Depth
  • RailHeight (Number): Rail height
  • HeaderDrop (Number): Header drop
  • HorizPattern (Text): Horizontal pattern
  • VertPattern (Text): Vertical pattern
  • MergeAdjacent (Boolean): Merge adjacent
  • FlipDir (Boolean): Flip direction
  • FirstBalcony (Boolean): First balcony
  • CornerWindow (Boolean): Corner window
  • CornerOffset (Number): Corner offset
  • SegLength (Boolean): Seg length

Outputs (8)

  • Slabs (Brep): Slabs
  • Railings (Brep): Railings
  • Partitions (Brep): Partitions
  • Glass (Brep): Glass
  • SolidPanels (Brep): Solid panels
  • HeaderPanels (Brep): Header panels
  • CornerPanels (Brep): Corner panels
  • Info (Text): Method, assumptions, simplifications and references.

Method, assumptions and simplifications

The same notes appear in the component's Info output.

Method

  1. Each boundary curve is one floor (height from Heights by path and index, default 4.0 m). Its segments are sorted into corner pieces and middle pieces.
  2. With SegLength, segments shorter than 2 × BayWidth become corner pieces. With CornerOffset > 0, each longer segment loses CornerOffset at both ends to corner pieces.
  3. Corner pieces become a full-height solid panel, or with CornerWindow glass to height - HeaderDrop plus a header panel.
  4. Middle pieces are divided into bays of about BayWidth (round(length / BayWidth) equal parts). Bays are numbered along the floor and the active horizontal pattern (chosen by the VertPattern digit for that floor) says which bays get a balcony (1).
  5. A balcony bay gets glass to height - HeaderDrop, a header above, a slab (straight loft between the bay line and its copy offset by Depth, FlipDir for the other side), a railing (the offset line extruded RailHeight) and side partitions of RailHeight. With MergeAdjacent, the partition between two neighbouring balconies is left out.
  6. Other bays get a full-height solid panel. With FirstBalcony False, the first curve of each branch (ground floor) has no balconies.
  7. The HUD reports glass, solid, header and corner areas (length × height), slab area (bay length × Depth) and the balcony count.

Assumptions

  • Bounds are horizontal lines or outlines at floor levels, in metres, listed from the ground floor up within each branch.
  • Defaults: BayWidth 3.0 m, Depth 1.5 m, RailHeight 1.1 m, HeaderDrop 0, HorizPattern 10, VertPattern 0; non-positive sizes fall back to defaults.

Simplifications and limits

  • All parts are zero-thickness surfaces; slabs have no thickness and railings are solid surfaces.
  • Offsets for curved bays use a coarse 0.1 tolerance; areas are length × size, not measured on the surfaces.

Workflow

  • Facade JSON Parser BoundsExt and Heights -> Bounds and Heights; outputs -> Custom Preview or bake.
