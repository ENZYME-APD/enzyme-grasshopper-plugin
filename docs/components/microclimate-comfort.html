Microclimate Comfort

Calculates the perceived 'Feels Like' temperature using Fast Apparent Temperature or Precise UTCI.

Site Analysis Nickname: FeelsLike

Overview

Calculates the perceived 'Feels Like' temperature using Fast Apparent Temperature or Precise UTCI.

Inputs (7)

  • Points (Point): Analysis points.
  • Air Temp (Number): Air temperature in Celsius.
  • Humidity (Number): Relative humidity in %.
  • Wind Speed (Number): Wind speed in m/s at 10m height.
  • Solar Factor (Number): Solar exposure from 0.0 (shade) to 1.0 (sun).
  • Method (Integer): 0 = Fast Apparent Temp, 1 = Precise UTCI
  • Run (Boolean): Optional. False = skip the analysis and keep showing the last results (cached). Share one toggle with the Analysis Dashboard to switch everything on/off together.

Outputs (6)

  • Points (Point): Analysis points.
  • Feels Like (Number): Perceived temperature in Celsius.
  • Category (Text): Comfort category label.
  • Colors (Colour): Gradient color mapping.
  • Dashboard Data (Text): JSON Legend Data
  • Info (Text): Method, assumptions, simplifications and references.

Method, assumptions and simplifications

The same notes appear in the component's Info output.

Method

  1. Each point takes Air Temp, Humidity, Wind Speed and Solar Factor from the lists by index (the last value is reused if a list is shorter; defaults 25 °C, 50 %, 1 m/s, 1.0).
  2. Method 0: Apparent Temperature AT = T + 0.33 e - 0.70 v - 4.0, e = RH/100 × 6.105 × exp(17.27 T / (237.7 + T)) hPa, plus 4 °C × Solar Factor.
  3. Method 1: mean radiant temperature is set to T + 15 °C × Solar Factor; UTCI is evaluated with a polynomial in T, v, Tmrt - T and vapour pressure (Magnus formula, kPa, capped at 5). T is clamped to -50..50 °C and v to 0.5..30 m/s.
  4. Categories: AT uses fixed bands (40, 32, 27, 20, 10, 0 °C); UTCI uses the standard stress bands from very strong heat (38+) to very strong cold (below -27).
  5. Colours run from dark blue at -10 °C to dark red at 40 °C through 5 fixed stops.

Assumptions

  • Inputs are point values for the same moment (one hour or design condition); the points only carry the results, the tool does not look at geometry.
  • Wind Speed is at 10 m height; Solar Factor 0-1 comes from a sun or shade study.

Simplifications and limits

  • Radiation is a fixed add-on (+4 °C AT, +15 °C Tmrt in full sun), not a computed mean radiant temperature.
  • No clothing, activity or local wind reduction; AT categories are not from a standard.

Workflow

  • Sun Hours Analysis exposure -> Solar Factor; wind results (Fast CFD) -> Wind Speed; EPW hour values -> Air Temp and Humidity.

References

  • Steadman R.G. (1994), Norms of apparent temperature in Australia, Australian Meteorological Magazine 43; the Australian Bureau of Meteorology apparent temperature formula.
  • Bröde P. et al. (2012), Deriving the operational procedure for the Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI), International Journal of Biometeorology 56.
